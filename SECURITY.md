# Security policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately. Do not open a public issue.

- On GitHub, open the repository's Security tab and choose "Report a vulnerability" (private vulnerability reporting).
- Or write to the privacy contact address: `confidentialite@<domaine>`. This is the `PRIVACY_CONTACT` address in `app/src/content/contact.ts`; the placeholder is replaced with the real address before launch.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think the impact is.

## What to expect

You will get an answer within 7 days. We will keep you informed while we fix the problem and will say when it is fixed.

## Rules for testing

- Test only with your own accounts and your own data.
- Do not access, change or delete other people's accounts or data.
- Do not disrupt the service (no denial-of-service or spam).
- Stop and report as soon as you find a way to reach someone else's data.
